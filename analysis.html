ⓘ Chooses which publication venues count in every Analysis tab. CSRankings Default uses its primary venues; CSRankings All also includes extended venues; CORE options use CORE conference tiers.
Trends ⓘ For a university, shows its US rank over time using a rolling 10-year window. For a researcher, shows yearly fractional publication activity. Example: a university moving from rank 20 to 15 rose five places; a researcher at 1.5 received 1.5 fractional publication credits that year.
Area Growth ⓘ Shows the selected university's or researcher's fractional publication credit in each research area by year. Example: Security at 2.5 in 2025 means the selected target received 2.5 combined fractional credits in that area that year.
Faculty Diversity ⓘ Measures how many active faculty published in at least two top-level areas within a rolling three-year window. Example: 40% means 4 of 10 active faculty published across two or more areas.
Publishing Effort ⓘ Compares the selected university's areas using fractional papers per active faculty per year. Example: 22 credits from 10 active faculty over 5 years gives an effort value of 0.44.
Conference Trends ⓘ Tracks the selected university's or researcher's yearly fractional credit at eligible conferences. The conference set above determines eligibility; this tab's checkboxes narrow it further. Example: compare whether the target's NeurIPS and ICML output rose or fell over the same years.
Collaboration ⓘ Shows coauthor-intensity and faculty-concentration proxies; it does not claim to identify cross-university collaborations. Example: 10 raw credits divided by 5 fractional credits gives a 2× team-size proxy.
Data Health ⓘ Checks venue mode, fractional-credit totals, ranking order, and source metadata coverage. Example: “Pass” with zero inconsistencies means the current CSPicks calculation satisfies its CSRankings compatibility checks.
