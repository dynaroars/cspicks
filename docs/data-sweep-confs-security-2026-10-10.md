Existing CCS 2028 skeleton remains unknown. CCS 2027 chairs were not verified; do not treat them as audit-confirmed.
- NDSS 2028 has estimated=true and verified=false locally. Its projected dates are not official announcements; no contradictory official schedule found. Preserve the estimation workflow while correcting the unrelated ndss.org link and clearly identifying the values as projections. No date-clearing proposal is made.
- NDSS 2026 separate abstract deadlines: NOT FOUND in CFP, which lists paper deadlines and does not announce a separate registration deadline. Proposals to clear the local assumed dates are needs-review.
- NDSS 2027 summer response/notification are explicitly tentative in CFP; fall response is November 11–13, notification November 24. Main event and chair metadata confirmed.
- IEEE S&P has both rebuttal text due dates and longer interactive periods. The scalar proposals for 2026 use the explicit text due deadline; interactive windows are documented in CFP. Existing 2027 Cycle 2 range can be preserved if the UI convention prefers windows.
- [NDSS 2026 proceedings](https://www.ndss-symposium.org/wp-content/uploads/NDSS2026_Proceedings_Front_Matter.pdf), PC message on PDF page 46, reports 1481 reviewed submissions, 265 accepted and 17.89%. The Internet Society message separately describes under 17% against almost 1600 overall submissions: denominator differs. Structured statistics use the explicit PC report denominator.
- [USENIX Security 2026 PC message](https://www.usenix.org/sites/default/files/sec26_message.pdf) reports 3028 raw submissions, 2750 valid submissions, 362 accepted, and 13.2% relative to valid submissions. Cycle 1: 1278 raw, 1181 valid, 166 accepted (14.1% valid); Cycle 2: 1750 raw, 1569 valid, 196 accepted (12.5% valid). Central reviewer must preserve the denominator in notes if adding statistics.
- Crypto/Eurocrypt 2026 acceptance statistics: NOT FOUND in edition home/CFP or [IACR acceptance table](https://www.iacr.org/cryptodb/data/acceptance.php), whose 2026 rows covered other venues. IEEE S&P and CCS 2026 acceptance statistics: NOT FOUND in audited edition/CFP/official-domain search; proceedings-front-matter research remains incomplete for those four venues. No guessed rates or totals proposed.
