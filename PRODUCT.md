# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Prospective PhD applicants navigating the PhD admission process in the US, searching for potential advisors based on research area and publication metrics, and comparing university department strengths.

## Product Purpose
Provide an intuitive, fast, and interactive explore-and-analysis interface for Computer Science professors, schools, and research areas, functioning as an accompanying tool to the PhD Demystify book.

## Brand Personality
Academic-modern: professional, structured, clean, and data-focused, yet fresh, modern, and engaging.

## Anti-references
- Generic, boring academic tables (e.g., 2012 internal web tools).
- Saturated SaaS-cream templates with tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows, glassmorphism, and identical cards.
- Overly loud, dark-theme tech dashboards with distracting neon gradients.

## Design Principles
- **Clarity Over Clutter**: Complex rankings and publication matrices must be represented with high scannability and minimal cognitive friction.
- **Academic Rigor, Modern Polish**: Preserve the authority and structured nature of the data while using modern layout rhythms, harmonized palettes, and Outfit/Inter typography.
- **Empowering Exploration**: Ensure interactive flows (like comparing schools or simulating ranking impacts) feel immediate, responsive, and easy to interpret.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Strict contrast ratios (≥4.5:1 for body copy).
- Clear screen reader scannability for interactive data tables.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion for all UI transitions.
